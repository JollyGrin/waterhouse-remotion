# Templates

Every template in this repo, by the name the team uses for it. Say the name
to Claude ("make the PullUp promo for Friday", "render the door stinger",
"use the Fuse intro") and it knows what you mean. Share these names in
WhatsApp so others can use the same thing.

When you make a new template, Claude names it, adds it here and tells you
the name. Rules for names and where things go: [AGENTS.md, "Templates"](AGENTS.md#templates-names-people-can-say).

To see any of them move: ask Claude to *"open Remotion Studio"* and click
the name in the list on the left.

## Event and session videos

Filled in automatically from the studio calendar and the portal.

| Name | What it is | Format | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PullUp** | 10-second "pull up tonight" promo for an event: the artist's photo, the time, a fake chat | 9:16 | "make the PullUp promo for Friday's event" |

| **WeeklyLineup** | this week's lineup as an Instagram story | 9:16 | "render the weekly lineup" |
| **HouseWeekly** | the weekly board: artists ranked by the crowd they brought, badges, next week's lineup | 9:16 | "make last week's board" |
| **ArtistRecap** | "who showed up": an artist's last shows, who came, who stayed, who was new | 9:16 | "make a recap for <artist>" |
| **SessionPoster** | square session cover with the guest's photo cut out (photo needs macOS 14+) | square | "make a poster for <guest>'s session on <date>, photo is <file>" |

| **SessionOpen** / **SessionClose** | first / last 5 seconds of an episode inside the Waterhouse mark | 16:9 | "make the logo reveal for the <guest> episode" |

## Stingers (OBS scene transitions)

About 4 seconds, see-through background, for OBS. Say "render the <name>
stinger".

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **door** | white paper card; a shadow grows under the door of the mark, knock knock, hand-held camera |
| **corner** | black card; the logo bounces into the top-left corner, flash, the glass cracks |
| **lamp** | a work lamp swings in on its cord; the mark only exists where its light falls |
| **paper** | stop motion from above: the pieces of the mark drop onto warm paper as black card |
| **neon** | night, rain on a wall; the mark is a neon sign striking tube by tube |
| **vhs** | a CRT; a hand brings in a labelled cassette |
| **fader** | a mixing desk from above; Denzo's hand pushes a fader up |
| **flap** | a split-flap departure board flips into the mark and spells WATERHOUSE |
